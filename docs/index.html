neuma

Gregorian chant, read from GABC notation and sung by AI voices.

The Advent hymn Rorate caeli desuper, complete: the refrain and all four verses. Four ElevenLabs voices, a few cents and milliseconds apart, in a generated church reverb.

How one voice is made

ElevenLabs has no endpoint that takes a melody, so the refrain goes through three stages. Listen in order.

Voices by elevenlabs.io. Chant transcription from GregoBase (CC0). Code, and notes on what worked and what did not: github.com/mkondratek/neuma.
